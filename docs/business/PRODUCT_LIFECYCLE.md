\# Product Lifecycle



\## Purpose



Products represent commercial items.



Examples:



\- ThinkPad L14 G4

\- Cisco Catalyst Switch

\- HP Monitor



\---



\## Lifecycle



Category

↓

Product

↓

Warehouse



↓



Sale



or



↓



Create Device



\---



\## Rules



Products are commercial objects.



Products can exist in warehouse stock.



Products can be sold.



Products can be transformed into Devices.



Products are not company assets.



After Device creation the Product stock is reduced.
