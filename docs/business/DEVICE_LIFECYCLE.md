\# Device Lifecycle



\## Purpose



Devices represent physical company assets.



Examples:



\- INV-00001

\- INV-00002

\- INV-00003



\---



\## Lifecycle



IN\_STOCK

↓

ASSIGNED

↓

RETURNED

↓

REPAIR



or



↓



RETIRED

↓

DISPOSED



\---



\## Rules



Devices are company assets.



Devices are assigned to employees.



Returned devices remain assets.



Devices are never converted back into Products.



Disposed devices remain available for audit history.
