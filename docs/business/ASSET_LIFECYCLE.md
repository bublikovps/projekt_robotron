\## Enterprise Rules



Products and Devices are separate entities.



A Product is a commercial item.



A Device is a company asset.



Creating a Device decreases Product stock.



Returned Devices remain Assets.



Disposed Devices are archived.



Devices are not returned to Product inventory.
